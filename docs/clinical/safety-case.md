# Clinical safety case and hazard log (framework)

**Status:** framework prepared from the build for the clinical safety officer and the clinical lead (spec 22, M6; DCB0129-style). Engineers do not rate clinical risk (spec 0 rule 5): every severity, likelihood and acceptability is `TODO_CLINICAL` until the clinical safety officer completes it. The controls listed are what the software does today, each with its test.

## Scope

Patient check-ins and red flags, verdicts (shadow mode only: never shown in care, ADR-0028), the clinician review and decision, the pre-surgery check, reminders, sharing and deletion.
